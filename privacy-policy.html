Privacy Policy

Last updated on 25th July 2026

At DigiDr, we prioritise the privacy and security of all doctors, clinics, and their patients.

Data Ownership

All data (doctor profiles, clinic details, patient information) belongs solely to the respective doctors/clinics. DigiDr will never sell or share this data.

Data Usage

Information is used only to provide DigiDr services — such as creating microsites and publishing content — with explicit user consent, that too from users' handles only.

Patient Data Privacy

Patient-related information (appointments, feedback, contact details) is stored securely and is accessible only to the respective doctor/clinic.

Security Measures

All data is encrypted, stored on secure servers, and protected using industry-standard security protocols.

Third-Party Sharing

DigiDr does not share any personal or clinic data with third parties without consent, except as required by law.

Compliance

DigiDr complies with applicable data protection laws (like India's DPDP Act and GDPR principles).

Your data stays yours. DigiDr only powers your presence — never compromises your privacy.
